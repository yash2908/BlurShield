Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

ScreenHush is a Chrome extension that blurs parts of websites (chats, emails, history) so they stay private while you share your screen, stream or work in public.

What we do with your data

ScreenHush does not collect, store on a server, sell or share your personal data.

  • Website content: ScreenHush adds blur styles to pages you choose. It does not read, copy, save or send the text of your messages, emails or pages anywhere.
  • Settings: your settings (which sites are on, blur strength, items you chose to blur) are saved only in your own browser.
  • History and bookmarks (optional, Pro): if you open the “Private history and bookmarks” page and allow access, ScreenHush reads your history and bookmarks only to show them to you on that page, blurred. This data never leaves your computer.
  • Payments and license keys: Pro is sold through Dodo Payments, which acts as the seller (Merchant of Record) and handles your payment and taxes. We never see your card details. After you buy, Dodo Payments emails you a license key. When you enter it, ScreenHush sends only that key to Dodo Payments to activate it, and checks it again about once a day so refunded keys stop working. Your license key is stored only in your browser. See the Dodo Payments privacy policy for how they handle payment information.

Permissions

  • Storage: saves your settings in your browser.
  • Access to WhatsApp Web and Gmail: to blur them.
  • Access to other websites (optional): only the sites you turn on in ScreenHush, or the site where you use “Blur something on this page”. Chrome asks you first each time.
  • Scripting: adds the blur to the sites you allowed.
  • Access to Dodo Payments servers: only to activate and check your Pro license key.
  • History and bookmarks (optional): described above. Chrome asks you first.

No tracking

ScreenHush has no analytics, no ads and no tracking. This website does not use cookies.

Children

ScreenHush is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
