What it does

Blurs private chats

Chat lists, names and older messages are blurred. Your newest 2, 3 or 5 messages stay readable for context.

Hover to reveal

Move your mouse over anything to read it. Press Alt+B to turn blur on or off instantly.

Private by design

Nothing you see is read, saved or sent anywhere. No tracking, no ads, no analytics.
